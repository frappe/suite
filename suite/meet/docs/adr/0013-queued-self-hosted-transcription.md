# Use queued self-hosted batch transcription

Meet needs a saved Transcript after the conversation, so capture audio and queue it for batch transcription on infrastructure we control. Use a shared multi-site queue and worker pool whose capacity can be sized independently of the live-caption service, rather than preserving live-caption output as the authoritative Transcript. This accepts delayed results and temporary audio storage in exchange for queued processing and independent inference deployment. Third-party transcription services are excluded. Target delivery within one hour after capture ends as an internal operating target; delete source audio after the usable complete or partial Transcript is delivered and retain failed jobs' audio for at most seven days for retries. Model selection and hardware sizing depend on pilot measurements; improved recognition accuracy must be validated rather than assumed. See [the design](../transcription-design.md) for the product boundaries.
